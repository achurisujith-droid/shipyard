Positioning

Not another prompt-to-app toy.

Shipyard is built for founders who have outgrown simple app builders. It standardizes planning, architecture, reusable backend capabilities, vendor setup, testing, deployment, monitoring, and human escalation so a product can converge toward a real production standard.

The system

One governed path from idea to production.

Shipyard separates judgment from generation: rules decide what is required, agents implement, verification proves progress, and developers step in when risk is too high.

Why it can work

The durable asset is not the model. It is the production layer around it.

Local agent orchestration

Shipyard can drive a local coding-agent session while keeping provider credentials inside the native tool.

Reusable capability supply chain

Authentication, billing, storage, audit logs, monitoring, and deployment become versioned skills with tests.

Independent verification

A feature is complete only when evidence passes, not when the coding agent says it is done.

Human-backed completion

When risk rises, Shipyard prepares a developer handoff with logs, failed tests, context, and acceptance criteria.

Revenue engine

Services appear when the product has evidence.

The upsell is contextual: payment reviews, monitoring setup, launch reviews, QA, security, and MVP completion are recommended only when readiness checks or incidents show a real need.
